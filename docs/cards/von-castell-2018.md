# von-castell-2018 — Paint the walls white (bright surfaces make a room look bigger)

- paper: von-castell-2018.txt
- citation: von Castell, Hecht & Oberfeld (2018). Bright paint makes interior-space surfaces appear farther away. PLoS ONE, 13(9), e0201976. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201976 (open access, CC BY)
- status: read

## Fit for Object (reviewer's judgment, not facts)
- Participant time: the original is very long (130 minutes, 10 repetitions of 48 conditions). A game version with a handful of rooms per condition would fit 5–10 minutes but would lose most of the averaging that makes a 7 cm effect visible.
- Task/tension: none; look at an empty room and estimate its width.
- Within-person reveal: in principle yes (all conditions are within each person, and the paper reports effects relative to each observer's own baseline). In practice a 7–8 cm shift on a 4.5 m room is too small for one player to find surprising, and with few trials it may not show at all.
- Works if the player expects tricks: probably; the estimate is perceptual, though a player who suspects paint matters may correct.
- Space tier: seated.
- VR or mixed reality: VR suits the original (a simulated empty room, viewed in a headset). Mixed reality would need repainting the player's real walls virtually, which the Quest browser cannot do reliably (room mesh unverified).
- Quest 3 feasibility: easy: rooms are simple boxes, brightness is a material change, estimates can be given with a slider or pointer instead of a spoken number (a change from the original). Head tracking would be on, unlike the chin-rest original; whether head movement changes the effect is untested.
- Replication: a series of studies from one lab (ceiling, walls) in headset simulations; no independent or real-room replication noted in the paper.
- Ethics: none.
- Verdict: reject for a room (effect is real within the lab's series but small and needs very many trials); at most a background manipulation inside another room, e.g. as a hidden variable in a space-perception room.
