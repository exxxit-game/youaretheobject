- citation: Otterbring, Bhatnagar & Folwarczny (2022). Selecting the special or choosing the common? A high-powered conceptual replication of Kim and Markus' (1999) pen study. The Journal of Social Psychology, published online 3 February 2022, doi:10.1080/00224545.2022.2036670.

## Facts
| What | Value | Quote (verbatim from the .txt) | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Participants | 729 adults recruited online (Prolific) | "Participants (N = 729, 55% female; Mage = 33.47 years, SD = 12.11) were recruited through Prolific." | 2 |
| Participants by country | 499 born in the US, 230 born in China | "Of the total sample, 499 were from the US, and 230 were from China." | 2 |
| Procedure | One imagined choice, described in text: four orange pens, one green | "Imagine there are five pens, of which four are orange and one is green." | 2 |
| Presentation | Text only, no real or pictured pens | "Participants were only presented with the different choice options in text rather than in actual, physical format." | 3 |
| Duration | Not stated; a single question | "Participants' pen choice (common vs. uncommon) served as the dependent variable." | 2 |
| Main result | US 62.53% chose the uncommon pen, China 50.00% | "The US participants showed a clear preference for the uncommon option (62.53%), whereas Chinese participants' choice pattern resembled the flip of a coin (50.00%)." | 3 |

## Fit for Object (reviewer's judgment, not facts)
- Participant time: seconds — one question.
- Task/tension: none; an imagined choice in words.
- Within-person reveal: none. A single binary answer; the only effect is a small difference between two countries.
- Works if the player expects tricks: irrelevant — nothing hidden to discover.
- Space tier: seated (online text question; no movement).
- Quest 3 feasibility: possible but pointless as a standalone room; VR adds nothing to a text question, and building real pens would be the 1999 study again, not this one.
- Replication: this paper is itself the replication of kim-markus-1999: direction confirmed, effect about a quarter of the original size.
- Ethics: none special.
- Verdict: reject — as a room it is a one-question survey with a small country-level effect that no single player can see; keep it as the replication evidence on the kim-markus-1999 card.
