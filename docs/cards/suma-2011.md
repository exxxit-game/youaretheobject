## Fit for Object (reviewer's judgment, not facts)
- Participant time: VR part not stated; 12 rooms of walking, likely 10–20 min.
- Task/tension: a simple errand (switch on screens); the surprise comes only in the reveal.
- Within-person reveal: yes — a replay from above can show the player's own path and the moving door.
- Works if the player expects tricks: unknown; Exp. 2 used a wide field of view to "break" it and it still held, but players of a psychology game will look for changes.
- Space tier: large 4.3 × 4.3 m (14 × 14 ft, real walking).
- Quest 3 feasibility: works only for players whose boundary is at least that big; Meta's roomscale minimum is 2 × 2 m, so most players cannot take it. A seated or turning-in-place version would be a different experiment and is not offered.
- Replication: no independent replication found in the paper; the paper itself is two studies by one group.
- Ethics: none special.
- Verdict: room (large space only) — not a first room, since most players lack the space; offered to players with a 4.3 × 4.3 m boundary.
