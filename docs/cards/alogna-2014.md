## Fit for Object (reviewer's judgment, not facts)
- Participant time: the RRR2 order needs about 26 min (video, 20-min filler, 5-min description, lineup) — too long for one room, but the filler could be another room of the same session: see the event first, play another room, then describe (or do the control task) and pick from the lineup.
- Task/tension: moderate — witness a theft, then make an identification with an honest "not present" option.
- Within-person reveal: weak — the effect is between groups and probabilistic (16 percentage points); one player gets one condition and only learns "right or wrong". It becomes visible only in statistics across players ("describers vs others").
- Works if the player expects tricks: moderately — the player does not know which condition is the manipulation; a player who knows about verbal overshadowing might describe half-heartedly.
- Space tier: seated (video on a screen, writing at a desk).
- VR or mixed reality: either — the original is a flat video. Staging the theft live in VR or in the player's own room (mixed reality) would be a new stimulus, not the replicated one; the original video and lineup are protocol materials on OSF (rights unchecked).
- Quest 3 feasibility: the lineup choice and confidence are recorded automatically by pointing. Writing for 5 min is impractical in a headset; a spoken description (microphone, not recorded or analysed) is the natural substitute, but spoken vs written overshadowing is not checked here — a design change. The 20-min gap must be filled by other game content.
- Replication: this paper is the replication — preregistered, 31 + 22 labs, robust −16% with the RRR2 order, small −4% with the RRR1 order. The original effect size was overestimated for one order, matched for the other.
- Ethics: a simulated bank robbery video — mild; give a short content note.
- Verdict: interlude — split around another room (event at the start, description and lineup after); the most solidly replicated effect in this area with an automatic measure, but between groups only and needing a 20-min delay.
