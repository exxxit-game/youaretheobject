Settled: never asked again, never advised against; a change comes only from the owner. One line a decision with
his key words (the date he said them, UTC); loaded with CLAUDE.md at every session start and after every compaction.
All his words, how each came, and the texts he accepted: docs/owner-decisions-history.md, read when a line is not
enough. A new decision: its line here, the rest there. A risk list or a general method is no reason to reopen one.

## Data, science, money
- The lever stays with us, we are our own lab, only how is open («Рычаг должен быть всегда у нас» 9.10); data protection first («это самое серьезное» 9.10); never sell data.
