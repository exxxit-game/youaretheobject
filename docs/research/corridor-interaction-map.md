# Connected interaction map, 9 November 2026

The owner prioritised relationships on the existing floor-0 blueprint: every
surface, fixture, movement and return should serve the shared purpose. This is
a bounded planning layer, not a new game scene or a claim of exhaustive research.
The [approved foundation](perception-corridor.md) and [room plan](corridor-room-plan.md)
remain authoritative. No chat history or source originals were deleted.

## One entrance

root of [the knowledge index](perception-knowledge.md). The same A2 PDF now has
the geometric plan on page 1 and seven route summaries on page 2.

- **Details:** 31 nodes, 51 directed relationships and 15 plan areas. A detail

- **Routes:** orientation/comparison; the first deal; return to consequences;
  N-to-N+/-1-to-N; unpaid investigation; interruption/resumption; future checkpoint.

The 18 existing inventory groups remain covered, including the combined V1/T1
sign. F1/F2/F3 floors are selectable too; overlapping stair components have a
shared entry and individual choices. Repeated tiles stay one parameterised type,
with individual cells available in the existing geometric data when needed.

## State and identity

The logical floor, displayed number, door, episode and scene version are distinct.
The current executable book episode is only `intro-book`: four offers
100/250/500/1000, three voluntary raises, accept/refuse/leave at any open price.
Entry, looking, bargaining and silence do not commit the act. Leaving an open
offer preserves its price; refusal and acceptance are terminal. A completed act
does not pay again through another floor, return, or reload.

Reload preserves the book, not the player's pose: the current preview starts
again at its old anchor on logical floor 0. A failed or invalid save stops the
deal. A general episode director, multiple working doors, cross-device saves,
the selected central start and combined sign are not implemented by this map.
The book contract is in [the existing preview note](../rooms/book-deal-preview.md).

## Mechanisms and limits

Geometric reinterpretation, scripted replacement, material changes, physical
lighting, rendered lighting, camera rotation and support/gravity changes remain
separate. The observer's position can permit a view without proving notice,
recognition, understanding or moral change. Open stair gaps and both eye views
can reveal a replacement. Reusing a room cannot erase episode history or replace
a simultaneously visible/heard space without an appropriate design.

Existing architecture/art/perception/light/route sources and creator interviews
are reused by ID; their reading scope and transfer limits travel with each link.
One added primary bridge is [Alais and Burr 2004](https://win.pisavisionlab.org/Downloads/AlaisBurr_Ventril.pdf),
DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2004.01.029. The helper read procedures, results and discussion
(printed 257-261); the lead checked the complete file and selected method/result/
discussion passages, not every graph. Brief blobs/clicks, binocular viewing at
40 cm, training and restricted auditory cues do not specify a Quest conflict
angle. Compare picture-only, sound-only and a congruent pair before a conflict.
Natural-listening generalisation is explicitly conditional in the paper.

The planning interface uses a parallel text list and relationship table, following
[W3C complex-image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/).
Large list controls complement exact plan targets, informed by
[W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html).
CSS pixels are not VR dimensions; no accessibility certification is claimed.
