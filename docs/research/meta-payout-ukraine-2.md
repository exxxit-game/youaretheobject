# Meta Horizon Store payouts in Ukraine (2): individual or FOP, TIN, W-8, bank, practice

Follows docs/research/meta-payout-ukraine.md (what Meta's pages say). Checked 2026-10-10.
Every claim has its link and a short quote; "not confirmed" = no source found. Quotes from Ukrainian
sources are English translations marked "(translated)"; Ukrainian terms are transliterated (FOP = sole
proprietor, RNOKPP = individual tax number, YeDRPOU = company register code, KVED = activity codes,
PKU = Tax Code, DPS = State Tax Service, IPK = individual tax consultation, YeSV = social contribution,
NBU = National Bank, KUpAP = Code of Administrative Offences). My own readings are marked "(my reading)".
Not tax advice: an accountant confirms before anything is typed into Meta. The owner's facts (coordinator,
2026-10-10): he has no FOP, he is a private individual now; which Meta organisation the game belongs to
is left for later.

## Parts
1. Individual vs FOP group 3: rates, military levy, filing, may FOP-3 take foreign store income

## Part 1. Tax Code of Ukraine (PKU), edition of 17.09.2026 (basis: law 4967-IX)
Source: https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 (full text downloaded 2026-10-10; quotes translated).
- Individual, foreign income: 170.11.1, foreign-source income goes into the annual taxable income of a
  recipient "who must file an annual tax return" (translated), at the 167.1 rate: "The tax rate is 18
  percent" (translated). Royalties, 170.3.1: taxed "under the rules for dividends" at the 167.5.1 rate
  (translated), and 167.5.1 is "18 percent - for passive income" (translated). Filing 49.18.4: the annual
  return "by 1 May of the year following the reporting year"; payment 179.7 "by 1 August" (translated).
- Military levy (section XX, subsection 10, item 16-1): 1.3(1) for individuals "5 percent of the object of
  taxation"; 1.3(3) for single-tax group 3 "1 percent of income determined under Article 292"; 1.16: the 5%
  applies to annual-return income accrued "starting from 1 January 2025" (all translated). After martial
  law the individual rate returns to 1.5% from 1 January after the third calendar year after it ends.
- So an individual: 18% + 5% = 23%, whether the payout is a royalty or other foreign income (my reading).
- FOP group 3, limit: 291.4(3) "income does not exceed 1167 minimum wages set by law on 1 January"
  (translated). Budget law 2026 (4695-IX), https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/4695-20 , Art. 8: minimum
  wage from 1 January 2026 "monthly - 8647 hryvnias" (translated). 1167 x 8,647 = UAH 10,091,049 a year
  (my arithmetic).
- FOP group 3, rate: 293.3 "3 percent of income" when registered for VAT; "5 percent of income" when VAT
  is included in the single tax (translated). Plus the 1% levy: 5% + 1% = 6% (my sum).
- FOP group 3, filing: 296.3 a quarterly single-tax return; 49.18.2 "within 40 calendar days" after the
  quarter; tax paid 295.3 "within 10 calendar days after the filing deadline"; the levy is shown "in the
  single-tax return" (16-1, 1.11) (all translated).
- FOP social contribution, YeSV law 2464-VI (edition of 26.01.2026), https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2464-17 :
  8(5) "22 percent" of the base; 7(1)(3) a simplified-system FOP sets the base himself, but the contribution
  "cannot be less than the minimum insurance contribution" (translated) = minimum wage x 22% (2(5)).
  22% x 8,647 = UAH 1,902.34 a month (my arithmetic).
- Royalties are NOT single-tax income: 292.1(1) "passive income in the form of interest, dividends,
  royalties" is not included (translated).
- NOT a royalty, 14.1.225: payments "for the purchase of copies of intellectual property objects, including
  in electronic form, for use by their function for end consumption or for resale", and for use of "a
  computer program" limited to the copies needed ("end consumer" use) (translated). (My reading: a store
  sale of a copy or add-on to an end user is sales income, not a royalty; the IPKs below read it so.)
- Who cannot use the single tax, 291.5.1: gambling and lotteries, currency exchange, excise goods,
  financial intermediation, and others; software, games and publishing are not in it, and no KVED code
  bars group 3 there. 291.5.7 bars non-residents only. Money settlements only, 291.6: "exclusively in
  monetary form, cash or non-cash" (translated).
- Currency and date, 292.5-292.6: foreign-currency income converted "at the official NBU exchange rate on
  the date the income is received" (translated); the date is the day the money arrives.
- VAT, 181.1: registration is compulsory above UAH 1,000,000 of taxable supplies in 12 months, "except a
  person who is a single-tax payer of the first to third group" (translated). 186.3(a): the place of supply
  of "property rights to intellectual property ... including under licence agreements" is where the
  recipient is registered (translated) (my reading: supplies to Meta Ireland are outside Ukrainian VAT; so
  Meta's VAT field stays empty in both cases).
- Pending: a Ministry of Finance draft (second version published 19 March, DOU, dated 20 March; year 2026
  by context) would make simplified-system FOPs VAT payers above UAH 4 million from 1 January 2027 (the article
  does not say it was adopted), https://dou.ua/lenta/news/tax-bill-second-attempt/ ; the first bill was
  "rejected in the first reading" on 10 March (translated). The Code text of 17.09.2026 still has 181.1's
  single-tax exception (adopted or not: not confirmed).
- KVED: names of 62.01, 58.21, 58.29 and the move to NACE 2.1-UA from 1 January 2027 (Derzhstat order 191 of
  28.10.2025) were seen only in search results: unverified.
- Diia, https://diia.gov.ua/services/reyestraciya-fop : registers a FOP and lets one "choose the general or
  simplified tax system" (translated); "free"; for group 3 the tax office registers the single-tax payer
  within 2 working days; signed with a personal electronic signature.
- Individual without FOP, risks: NBU account rules (Resolution 162 of 2022, edition of 09.05.2025),
  https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/v0162500-22 , item 10: an individual "opens separate accounts for
  entrepreneurial, independent professional activity and for own needs"; "It is prohibited to use current
  accounts of individuals opened for own needs for operations connected with entrepreneurial ... activity"
  (translated). KUpAP Art. 164, https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/80731-10 : "conducting economic activity
  without state registration" is fined "from one thousand to two thousand tax-free minimum incomes"
  (translated; the print page showed edition 07.05.2017, current wording not re-checked). Whether regular
  store sales by an individual count as entrepreneurial activity: no DPS position found (not confirmed).

## Part 1. DPS individual consultations (IPK) on store and platform sales
- IPK 29.09.2026 No 5778/IPK/99-00-24-03-03 (FOP group 3 selling own digital works through a foreign
  platform, paid via Payoneer to a USD account), as reported by 7eminar.ua 05.10.2026,
  https://7eminar.ua/news/24962-fop-prodaje-cifrovi-produkti-cerez-platformu-nerezidenta-yak-dps-viznacaje :
  for copies for end consumption "such payment is not a royalty and is included in the single taxpayer's
  income"; a royalty "is not included in the single taxpayer's income" (translated). IPK text not opened
  (register https://cabinet.tax.gov.ua/registers/ipk is a search form, not typed into). An IPK binds only
  the payer who asked (general rule, not re-checked).
- IPK No 452/IPK/99-00-24-03-03 (published 26.01.2024; FOP group 3, non-VAT, apps sold through Apple as
  "agent for delivering licensed apps to end users"), as reported by
  https://zvit.vchasno.ua/taxes-consultation/iedynyy-podatok-fop-prodazh-dodatkiv-cherez-apple-1228071/ :
  the single-tax income is "the full amount including that commission" (gross, before the store's cut);
  money credited to accounts opened for personal needs is taxed as an individual's income (translated).

## Part 1/2. Meta's agreement: agent, licence, entity (DDA, effective May 9, 2025)
https://developers.meta.com/horizon/policy/developer-distribution-agreement/
- 2.4: "Developer hereby appoints MPT as Developer's authorized agent"; 2.1(a) MPT may "sell or otherwise
  distribute licenses to Product(s)"; 2.1: the licence to Meta is "fully-paid up, royalty free,
  non-exclusive"; 3.2: end-user licences "will be deemed to be granted by Developer".
- 1.10 Net Revenues = "all gross revenues received by MPT or its Affiliates from the sale of licenses",
  less taxes, processing fees, refunds; 5.4(a) MPT collects and remits "Transaction Taxes" (incl. "value
  added"); 5.3 each payment comes "together with a report showing the calculation of Developer Revenue"
  (the paper for a bank or accountant); invoices are not mentioned.
- Counterparty in the preamble: "Meta Platforms Technologies Ireland Ltd., located at Merrion Road, Dublin
  4" (Meta's FAQ says "Facebook Technologies Ireland Limited"; same company renamed: my assumption).
- (My reading) Meta sells as the developer's agent, like Apple in IPK 452: the payout is sales income for
  the single tax, not a royalty, and the base may be the gross price before Meta's 30% (and, unclear,
  before the taxes Meta deducts).

## Part 2. TIN and US forms
- OECD CRS TIN sheet, https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/ukraine-tin.pdf :
  an individual gets "a registration number of the taxpayer's account card ... (RNOKPP), which is also a tax
  number (TIN)"; "RNOKPP is the ten-digit numerical code"; "EDRPOU Code is the eight-digit numerical code"
  for "all legal persons incorporated in Ukraine". No separate TIN for individual entrepreneurs.
  PKU 70.12: RNOKPP is used by banks and FOPs "in all documents containing information about objects of
  taxation of individuals" (translated). (My reading: Meta's TIN field = the 10-digit RNOKPP whether he is
  a private individual or a FOP; YeDRPOU only for a company. Not confirmed by a Meta page.)
- W-8BEN, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben (rev. 10/2021): "You must give Form W-8BEN ... if you are
  a nonresident alien"; 6a "the foreign tax identifying number (FTIN) issued to you by your jurisdiction of
  tax residence"; line 10 "must be used only if you are claiming treaty benefits that require that you meet
  conditions" (also used for business profits without a US permanent establishment, with the article).
- W-8BEN-E, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8bene (rev. 10/2021): not for "a nonresident alien
  individual. Instead, use Form W-8BEN". (My reading: a FOP is the same person, so W-8BEN in both cases.)
- Meta, https://developers.meta.com/horizon/resources/publish-account-management-bank-tax/ (updated
  2026-05-13): payouts fail when "Your Tax Identification Number (TIN) does not match the records associated
  with your business entity" or "The name on your bank account does not match the name on your Meta
  developer account"; business accounts must match the registered business name, personal accounts the
  holder's name.
- US-Ukraine convention (1994), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/ukrain.pdf : Art. 7(1) business profits
  "shall be taxable only in that State unless" there is a US permanent establishment; Art. 12(2) royalty tax
  "shall not exceed 10 percent of the gross amount"; 12(3) royalties include "computer programs"; 12(5)(a)
  royalties "arise in a Contracting State when the payer is ... a resident of that State". (My reading: from
  Meta Ireland, no US-source royalty; the 10% / Art. 12 or 0% / Art. 7 question matters only if the payer is
  Facebook Technologies, LLC. Meta's FAQ: "Meta does not withhold any developer income tax".) Which article
  Meta's form asks for: not confirmed.

## Part 3. Bank and currency control (texts downloaded from zakon.rada.gov.ua 2026-10-10)
- Currency law 2473-VIII (edition of 26.06.2026), https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2473-19 : 13(1) the
  NBU "may set settlement deadlines for export and import operations of goods"; 13(2) export proceeds "are
  to be credited to the residents' accounts in Ukrainian banks", counted for "works, services, intellectual
  property rights" "from the day an act, invoice or other document confirming their provision is drawn up"
  (translated). Art. 1: "goods" as in the Foreign Economic Activity law (my reading: includes IP rights).
- NBU Resolution 18 (edition of 17.09.2026), https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/v0018500-22 : 14-2 "settlement
  deadlines for export and import operations of goods are 180 calendar days" (translated). No mandatory sale
  of export proceeds in its text (searched; not found).
- NBU Instruction, Resolution 7 of 2019 (edition of 31.05.2025), https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/v0007500-19 :
  6(1) supervision of an export of "works, services, intellectual property rights" not yet paid; 9(1) it ends
  when the unsettled sum "does not exceed the insignificant amount" (translated), tied to AML law 361-IX Art.
  20(1), https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/361-20 : "400 thousand hryvnias" (translated).
  (My reading: Meta's monthly payout within 30 days meets the 180-day deadline; small months are not
  supervised at all.)
- Account: a FOP needs a separate business account (NBU Resolution 162, item 10, Part 1 above). The Latin
  holder name a Ukrainian bank shows for a FOP USD account ("FOP ...", "PE ..." or the plain name), which
  must match Meta's account name: not confirmed (no bank page found; read it from the bank app).
- Bank papers: a DOU accountant (thread below) says the platform's public offer is enough and invoices are
  needed only "when there is no contract with the customer" (translated). Each bank's list: not confirmed.

## Part 5. Verdict (from the sources above; readings marked)
| | Private individual (his status now) | FOP group 3, non-VAT |
|---|---|---|
| Tax | 18% + 5% levy = 23% of payouts (167.1/167.5.1, 16-1) | 5% + 1% levy = 6% of income (293.3, 16-1); base gross or net of Meta's 30%: open |
| Social | none found for this income (not checked) | YeSV min UAH 1,902.34/month (my arithmetic) |
| Filing | one annual return by 1 May, pay by 1 Aug | quarterly return within 40 days + pay within 10 more |
| Limit | none | UAH 10,091,049 a year in 2026 (my arithmetic) |
| Risk | personal-account ban for business (NBU 162); unregistered-activity fine (KUpAP 164) if sales count as business | royalty reading takes income out of the single tax (292.1); 2027 VAT draft |
| Meta fields | name as in his tax record; TIN = RNOKPP; VAT empty; W-8BEN | same name and RNOKPP (my reading); VAT empty; W-8BEN; payout to the FOP USD account |
| Setup | none | Diia, free; group 3 within 2 working days; USD business account |

(My reading) Tax details "cannot be changed" in Meta afterwards (part 1 file), so the status is chosen
before the form: if a FOP is coming, register it first, then type the same name and RNOKPP into Meta.
