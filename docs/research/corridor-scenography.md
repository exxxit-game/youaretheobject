## What the sources support

- [Appia's original edition](https://archive.org/details/loeuvredartvivan00appirich)
  connects moving bodies, spatial forms and light. Selected text and explanatory
  leaves were read, not the whole illustrated book.
- [Craig's patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1022020A/en) describes
  reconfigurable monochrome screens, supports and position marks.
- [National Theatre's Ocean pack](https://images.nationaltheatre.org.uk/uploads/2022/12/OCEAN-TOUR_Education_Pack.pdf)
  explains puppetry, simple scenery, lighting and the effect of audience layout.
- [Mere Phantoms](https://www.merephantoms.com/infinite-fragilities/) connects
  discarded cardboard, paper sculptures, shadows and collaborative performance.
- [MoMA PS1](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/4798) describes rubbish
  assemblies producing portrait shadows under designed illumination.
- [Smithsonian Exhibits](https://exhibits.si.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Guide-to-Exhibit-Development.pdf)
  links purpose, message hierarchy, objects, media and reflection.

The full cards preserve each reading boundary and the distinction between a
source fact, our application and an untested effect. Shadow geometry, object
anamorphosis, projection, reflection, refraction and diffraction are separate.
A single lamp does not imply a single eye position. RGB imagery does not establish
spectral simulation. Physically simple material does not establish cheap rendering.

## Existing requirements and the real gap

The [book contract](../rooms/book-deal-preview.md) and `src/preview/deal-state.js`
already define bounded quotes, terminal refusal, preserved unfinished quotes and
one payment. Theatre sources are analogies for reuse, not proof of game storage.

Missing for this specific scene: one coherent artistic treatment, attention from
reachable views, light/sound cues, residual consequences and a measured device
cost. The study's G1-G8 rows distinguish existing work from those open questions.
Using the whole space does not require activating every effect simultaneously;
quiet, empty surfaces and stable landmarks can carry deliberate roles.

Proposals A/B/C examine a small object with a large shadow, reconfigurable panels,
and the familiar book room with changed relationships. They are not accepted
alternatives or an exhaustive style menu. C retains the selected first episode.

The next visible result is a concrete artistic sketch of C with entrance, close
and return views, a small palette and distinct states. The present functional
sheet is not that finished sketch. Exact positions and clearances require the
ordinary B19/B17 base; no invented dimensions or new input gestures are approved.
