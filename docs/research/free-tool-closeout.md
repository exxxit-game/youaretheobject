### Scope filter

Excluded corporate analytics, another task manager, another knowledge store and an always-running AI watcher:
no concrete next-room gap supports adopting them. OpenAlex, ResearchRabbit and Semantic Scholar are not full
Scite replacements: finding citation links differs from classifying citation context. Neither a graph nor a
classification replaces reading actual methods and results.

### Object filter

The assistant operates free sources; the owner is not asked to learn them. Do not create accounts merely to
duplicate objekt-files collections. Context7 examples require pinned-version checks. A personal trial can reject
a boring game experience but cannot establish a general psychological effect. A short room needs the original
procedure and a justified transfer. Local F: restoration is not independent proof of a cloud disaster recovery.

### Conclusions

| Strength or failure risk | Operational assistance | Remaining human/assistant responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Owner knows intent and experiences boredom, credibility and comfort | Short sequential Quest trials and feedback | Tests cannot replace taste; no revealing the experiment before play |
| Assistant can research and implement but can confidently be wrong | Research Methodology, originals, Europe PMC/OpenAlex, Crossref, required room-paper review | Read methods and opposing evidence; no abstract/count-only verdict (F1-F6) |

| Stale examples or invented library APIs | Context7 plus local pinned A-Frame 1.8.0 docs/source | Check version and behavior, especially master results (F8) |
| Limited subscription and expensive rework | Agreed model matrix, ordinary programs for mechanics, saved evidence | No measured savings percentage or unlimited AI work; no new paid service needed now (F9-F10) |
| Losing current changes | Git ancestry plus working-file snapshot and disposable restore | Old backups/sync do not establish current recovery; scope in receipt (F11) |

Choose existing papers/PubMed first; Europe PMC/OpenAlex extend discovery, Crossref supplies update/retraction
leads, Semantic Scholar is a reserve. ResearchRabbit remains an optional visual website. Do not buy Scite.
No new mandatory connector was identified for the first trial (F1-F10). New external spending for this selection: $0.
