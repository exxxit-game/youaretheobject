## Recommended presentation profiles, not accepted product decisions

| Device | Entry | Input and separate checks |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | Direct experience first; optional corridor later if it adds value | Touch selection and cancellation, rotation/zoom, interruptions, readable controls; no compulsory phone motion |
| PC | Corridor game; direct episode for an isolated experience or comparison | Keyboard/trackpad/mouse, grounded movement and separate looking/acting; pointer lock optional, with exit |
| Quest | Corridor and spatial room | Controller actions and reachable controls; seated/standing, both eyes, real projections, recenter, pause, performance; actual session required |
| TV | Initially a PC display is a separate profile; standalone TV browser remains a candidate | Actual model/year/browser, remote focus, OK/Back, held-button handling, distance/readability; casting is not local TV execution |

Mobile corridor navigation is technically possible; mandatory navigation is a
design cost, not a platform impossibility. Direct entry preserves access to the
content but removes the corridor's movement/memory context. Do not claim identical
experience, pool results, award route progress or repeat payouts implicitly.

Three contrasting transfer probes are candidates, not three new approved rooms:
the existing book for action/persistence; Theseus for sequence/interpretation;
blind spot as a limit case. Six Theseus parts are our conditional drawing, not
an original six-part psychological experiment. Moire is an additional promising
flat geometry candidate; texture filtering, scale and viewing angle require checks.
The [Exploratorium blind-spot procedure](https://www.exploratorium.edu/snacks/blind-spot)
depends on monocular viewing, fixation and card positioning. The same CSS percentages
or virtual distance do not establish the same retinal stimulus in a headset.

## Official sources and acceptance

[WebXR](https://www.w3.org/TR/webxr/), CR Draft 29 October 2026, separates session
support, actual entry, eye views and session visibility. A supported-mode query
does not guarantee a successful session. `visible-blurred` stops XR input and may
throttle frames; `hidden` stops input and XR frame callbacks. Page visibility is
separate. [XAUR](https://www.w3.org/TR/xaur/) is an informative Working Group Note,
not a certification checklist. Its navigation and alternative-input needs inform
the proposed profiles.

The ledger also records MDN Pointer Events, Pointer Lock, Gamepad, Page Visibility
and localStorage. Save committed transitions; interruption is not refusal/consent.
Local storage is per origin/browser and may fail, not a cross-device account.
[Samsung's table](https://developer.samsung.com/smarttv/develop/specifications/web-engine-specifications.html)
distinguishes model-year engines; it does not establish our application on all TVs.

Before a port: classify its type; identify exact source version; compare instructions,
materials, conditions, timing, controls, incentives and outcomes; retain explicit
inapplicable items; implement one adapter; check cancellation/repeat/return; perform
source and independent review; then test the actual target device. A philosophical
question or mathematical demonstration must not become a fabricated experiment.
