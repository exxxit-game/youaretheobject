## Approved foundation, 7 November 2026

Status: approved by the owner in the current conversation. His latest request
explicitly fixes the plan as the continuing reference and asks to keep working in
this same chat. Earlier labels such as proposed, unresolved purpose or held for a
purpose recheck describe the history below, not the status of this foundation.
They do not approve every former assistant suggestion or unresolved room detail.

**Purpose.** The corridor is our Flatland: through their own movement, attention,
choices and remembered experience, people can discover other readings of a
familiar world and of themselves. The owner's spiritual purpose is returning
people to God through personal experience, beauty, nature and human knowledge.
Preserve that authorial purpose without presenting an optical effect as scientific
proof of God, physical two-dimensionality, a fourth dimension or a new law.
The work invites discovery and compassion; an answer in a fictional game does
not diagnose a person's soul or establish a lasting change in them.

**Place and perception.** Start at an ordinary door, a small corridor and its turn
toward the stair. Build one recognisable floor N with ascent and descent, repeated
as logical N+1 and N-1 routes that can continue. The compact place supports many
readings; no castle or millions of separately modelled floors are required.
Tiles, rails, walls, doors, pictures, light and shadow, occlusion, parallax and
spatial sound participate through their relationships across reachable views.
Account for eye height, both eyes, attention, motion and memory. The surprise
accumulates as earlier interpretations change, rather than relying on one shock.

The moving-pattern core starts with **one displayed image**. The owner's reported
two perceived images are distinct from two copies deliberately drawn by the
illustration tool. His image of a patterned tower is a perceptual/artistic reference,
not an instruction to replace the stairwell with a new tower scene. Rotation,
scale, light and the observer work together. The owner's short anchor is
"I - light - movement"; it is not a complete mathematical model of perception.
Calculations, conditional blur and image overlays do not establish what a human
observer experiences or that a previously unknown mechanism has been discovered.

**Rooms and choices.** A small set of accessible rooms offers short text choices,
a stated fictional payment and yes/no answers. Begin with apparently small acts
and increase the demands and stakes gradually; the former example of opening
with killing a family is superseded. Physical depictions of violence are not
required. A refusal is valid and lets the person continue exploring. Bargaining
is optional, only after voluntarily expressed interest, never a loop forcing a
refusing player to say yes. Record accepted fictional deals and their payments.
Self-directed disgust and harm to others are different kinds of acts; sharing a
money ledger does not make them morally equivalent. Rooms may also contain
connected perceptual discoveries. Exact prompts and their order are still open.

**Money room and continuation.** This is a checkpoint, not necessarily the end of
the whole game. Show the recorded choices and balance and materialise that money
in the same small room. Zero balance produces no cash pile; a small balance
produces few bills and cannot justify burying the character. A very large
accumulation can fill the room and bury/kill the character. A surviving character
can continue exploring; a buried character restarts. Do not silently substitute
a different ending. Restart does not establish repentance, nor survival goodness.

**Next design work.** Develop the room contents and sequence, the meaning of yes
within the fiction, optional bargaining controls, stopping and winnings rules,
denominations/volume and the filling threshold, and what resets between attempts.
The player retains their own experience across returns. These details must fit
the approved purpose and compact space. Keep earlier capture, source inventory,
geometry and desktop/mobile/Quest verification as supporting work; choose their
order by the actual dependency of the next agreed detail. Approval of this plan
does not claim completed gameplay, human testing, a verified headset scan or release.

**Before acting.** Use existing human research and reusable project work first.
State the contribution to the goal, evidence and remaining uncertainty; weigh the
benefit against complexity, future consequences and the owner's time/energy;
choose the simplest adequate step and its observable success check. Complete
technical preparation before requesting the owner's effort or headset use.
Keep material decisions visible, without requiring a new checklist for every click.
Continue in the current chat; the owner changes the foundation, not an assistant's
unannounced reinterpretation. New evidence can justify a clearly explained revision.

experience moving beyond a familiar way of seeing, as in Flatland, encounter a
previously unavailable viewpoint, and return with a wider understanding.
Researching regularities supports this aim. A well-made stairwell alone does not
establish that the transition was experienced. Its precise construction remains open.

The owner now names the authorial purpose explicitly as returning people to God
through their own experience of nature, mathematics, physics and attentive discovery.
He describes his own encounter as direct personal knowledge, rather than accepting
someone else's words. Preserve this distinction when describing his intention.
The corridor's composition is still to be tested; spiritual outcomes are not established
by the route tests or the optical calculations.

The current vehicle is a player leaving an ordinary door to go up one floor. The central
floor N has an ascent and a descent that can continue. Familiar details invite an
initial interpretation. Movement reveals another reading of the same construction.
Further discoveries reinterpret earlier details. On returning, the player carries
the acquired experience and can investigate the familiar place differently.

Accumulating connected discoveries is the current interpretation of the intended surprise.
The example of a tree reading as a sun and a cat expresses connected interpretations
of one thing. The actual multi-view form still needs design and perceptual checks.
The N route and the repeated number are infrastructure and a diagnostic comparison.
Success is a player voluntarily using another view, explaining the discovery, or
finding the revealed depth again. A displayed memory counter cannot establish that.

The design combines geometry, occlusion, light, movement, sound and memory in one
walk. Each detail can serve as a credible landmark, a cue to another detail, part
of a shared image, or a means of revealing its construction. Record that connection
before adding the detail. Later changes use the same goal and may revise the means.

The owner's permanent working principle joins source-based thinking with action
planning. Use accumulated human knowledge and existing evidence, check applicability,
then choose a useful step, its consequences and verification. Label hypotheses where
the evidence leaves gaps. Complete preparation before requesting the owner's effort.

## Purpose recheck after the early chat, 7 November

The owner now questions whether added complexity has displaced the intended idea.
The [shared chat](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeedc7a-5904-83eb-a543-ea8f586a4910)
opened in Chrome. Its sidebar led to the original conversation, where earlier
messages were read back to the initial available discussion of emergence and
Hayakawa, before the stairwell photographs. This extends the old five-pair preview.
It is not a claim to have recovered preceding Tetris/Flatland exchanges elsewhere.

Primary owner anchors are his request not to repeat existing discoveries, his
explicit yes to discovering a regularity and letting others discover it through
play, and his distinction between a creator's rich possibilities and a player's
tedious exhaustive search. Later anchors are line/surface/height, the return after
0/90/180 degrees with retained experience, and the insistence that the stairwell's
parts interact together. The 0/1, mirrored-world and simulation ideas were exploratory;
they do not establish physical dimensionality, consciousness or a unified theory.

Two questions were entangled: how simple interacting rules produce complexity,
and how limited observations plus history produce an understanding of a world.
The current corridor concretely addresses the second. That does not demonstrate
that the first research programme has been fulfilled or that a new law was found.
The older assistant's proposed experiments are distinct from the owner's choices.

The implementation has route checks, placeholders and references. It has no tested
joint visual construction demonstrating the proposed change on returning, and no
specific open question whose novelty has been established against the literature.
More detail does not resolve these gaps. Scene expansion was held for that purpose
recheck; the approved foundation above now resolves it. Existing work and source
capture preparation are retained.

A candidate question is which minimal coupled geometry, illumination and viewing
conditions support a change of interpretation, and what survives at a comparable
return view after revealing movement. This is a proposal, not an agreed final
mission or a claim of scientific novelty. Its next design record must specify
the initial view, revealing action, return, observable result and comparison with
existing methods. It must preserve a satisfying short discovery rather than asking
players to search thousands of unsuccessful combinations.

After this review the owner clarified that the question is still how to move a
person as Abbott did in Flatland, expanding the ability to see from another
viewpoint. This resolves the priority for current work: the experience of a wider
frame of observation. The earlier search for regularities serves that experience;
it does not require claiming a new scientific law before the game can have meaning.
An initial spatial reading, a meaningful transition and recognition on return need
one connected design. Additional views and more objects alone do not establish it.
The [book's dedication](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/201/201-h/201-h.htm) explicitly
speaks of enlarging imagination; that passage was checked, not the entire book here.

The owner then made the vehicle explicit: the corridor itself is our Flatland.
Walking supplies the discovery, without requiring explanatory words. Ascending,
descending, continuing or returning should expose connected visual readings and
invite new questions. Placement calculations therefore concern relationships
between the same details across successive views, including their reverse order.
An alignment that resolves from one view and reveals its depth from another can
participate in that sequence. No completed composition is claimed by this example.

## Connected first composition

Candidate sequence for the ordinary first turn and its next landing.

1. The door and cabinet establish a specific place before any unexpected reading.
2. Floor seams and the corner bring a rail and distant frame into the same view.
3. A reachable side movement separates the contours and exposes their depths.
4. The next landing repeats enough landmarks to question the inference about place.
5. Returning lets the player independently find the revealing movement again.

For each detail record its first reading, linked neighbours, revealing action,
resulting reading, and what should remain recognisable on return. Preserve the
world geometry when assessing an opening revealed by movement. Deliberate changes
of a label or image have their own comparison conditions. Layers can coexist in
the eventual walk while isolated comparisons diagnose their contributions.
