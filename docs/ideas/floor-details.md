# Small details that add up, floor by floor (the owner's idea, 10.10)

His words in English (the Russian is in his message log; the board, docs/board.md, points here). It sits
beside his model of the game, Guy Ritchie's "Revolver": a game played again and again to be understood,
built detail by detail (.claude/rules/game.md). Not for now: the floors after the corridor.

## Quality is many small things
"Even a small thing you go and change is already a big change, if small thing after small thing after small
thing is gathered everywhere: such small things are exactly what makes the quality far better than
everyone else's. Most things are more or less average, but there is always someone who has something
better, because he tried hard everywhere all the time, and there is the worse, in the simplest words."

## Door numbers that stop making sense
"On the first floor there are just numbers. A couple of floors can be just numbers too, and then on some
floor the numbers go in order and the order may break. Going into the 900s is not interesting, honestly.
We go 100, 110, up to 150; after 150 it reaches 155, then comes room 1000, say, then room 3x, then a room
with a rainbow painted on it. That is where the filling starts: every floor filled bit by bit with all
sorts of different moments, details thrown in little by little, so that a person keeps thinking, comparing
and building different theories. As with Limbo: people made up so much about that boy and that girl, a
whole epic; I was shocked. I cannot do that, but people can. I will be interested to see how they behave
here. And afterwards I will explain that it was made exactly for this."
