# The owner's own experiment ideas

His words in English (the Russian is on the board, docs/board.md, and in his message log). He wants our own
new experiments run live with players, besides the re-created ones (docs/owner-decisions.md). Each is built on
the closest published studies, read in full; the research is done (docs/research/own-experiments-now.md: ten
candidates, this one among them) and the candidates wait for his pick.

## Two phones, two prices (10.10)
"Two phones lie on a table with a price sign beside each: one a very expensive phone, ten thousand
dollars, the other ten dollars. Which will you take? It is a virtual phone, not a real one, yet I think
people will take the expensive one. What interests me is how they will justify their choice: that they
can call better with it."

The interest is the reason given after the choice, not the choice. Published work to find and read before
any design (names to check, nothing taken from memory yet): reasons people give for a choice they did not
make for those reasons (Nisbett & Wilson, "Telling more than we can know", 1977); choice blindness
(Johansson et al. 2005, card docs/cards/johansson-2005.md); price as a cue to quality.

## Time that runs away (11.10)
"We tell a person that this will take a minute, and five pass, and then we ask whether he noticed. It has to
be so that he was caught up in something and did not notice." The published work on how long a stretch of
time feels when one is absorbed in a task, and on judging a span afterwards against judging it while waiting,
is to be found and read before any design; no names taken from memory yet.

## The scientists' tests, made more exact (11.10)
"Improve the scientists' tests. Think what they missed before. How to make them very exact? For example, how
long the decision took." What the headset can measure that the original lab could not (the time to each
answer, where his head was turned (Quest 3 has no eye tracking: docs/headset-capabilities.md), how he moved) is the start; each room's paper says what it measured.
