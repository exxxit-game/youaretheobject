## Deviations and our choices
This is explicitly a short local trial, not a diagnostic test, registered study or replication
of the paper's age/context hypothesis. No age/culture inference. Original 86 recruited,
76 analysed after exclusions; no participant average is fabricated from the graph.
The poor-context outward condition did NOT show the expected group effect (p. 5);
inward underadjustment and individual variability are described without a promised outcome.
No claim that a single player's missing effect invalidates the literature.

Paper monitor luminance was calibrated (p. 3); headset luminance is not calibrated.
RGB yellow/white/black, controller mechanics, headset optics, viewing distance and two-configuration
subset are deviations. Fin line width, panel dimensions and input rate are our rendering choices.
No demographic questionnaire or science consent because no data is sent for research.
Practice can repeat, unlike the once-only source practice. Recentring, leaving and pausing
are product controls; an interrupted measured adjustment restarts with its same initial value.
The reveal offers actual lengths without fins for inspection, after all measured answers.
The reveal states the major transfer differences; full geometry/practice/host details stay here.
The adjustable range is our 0-25 degree screen limit, not an original range restriction:
the paper specifies 0-21.5 only for initial lengths. Boundary answers are marked as limited.
The brief opening calls this a VR trial; no limited answer becomes proof of an illusion magnitude.

## Flow and measures
Intro -> practice instructions -> 2 practice adjustments -> repeat practice / start measured
-> 4 measured adjustments -> no-blame interference choice -> results -> explanation -> source -> inspect / restart.
Inspection returns to results. Menu reset/stop ask confirmation; stopped UI follows seated eye height.
No forced wait, timer, fixed completion deadline, scoring or correct-answer feedback during measure.
One explicit confirmation per adjustment. Menu/hidden headset cannot submit answers.
Leaving cancels the run. Re-entry starts afresh. Restart remains the same experiment identity.
Log: condition, practice flag, start/adjusted length and active adjustment time. No names/signatures.
Report excludes practice; mean adjusted length per condition, then (mean-reference)/reference.
No exclusions based on desired sign. Incomplete/malformed logs have no valid report.
Results stay in memory; no result/issue/comparison/upload calls. Session begins with recording false.
No original-group numeric comparison: source means are only plotted and no extracted values verified.
No sharing during this local prototype. Prior-experience questionnaire and research plan are deferred;
this trial cannot count as science data. Owner feedback is recorded only when actually supplied.
