# The tandem: who takes what, and what holds it

Loaded with CLAUDE.md at every start. His task of 15.10: split the work between him, me, us both and the
other tools, knowing that I err and he has no technical knowledge. The audit that day found the split of
14.10 right, but held by text that never loaded and by his clicks on what he cannot judge, with no visible
work to measure it (docs/research/process-audit-2026-10-15.md).

| Who | Takes | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| He | what the thing should be; yes or no to what he sees; stop; logins, money, the headset | his eyes |
| I | code, sources, counts, a picture of the thing before he sees it | not my word |
| The machine | what loaded into the window; his measure; tests; the process size; the stop before the headset; keys; main | code |
| Outside eyes | CodeRabbit on every pull request, closing its own remarks; Qwen Coder on a large code change; experienced people at a real dead end | themselves |
| Both | what comes next (he picks from plain options); showings (I look first); the board, his words verbatim | his word |

His measure: the features he accepted with his yes in 7 days, counted from git and shown to him at a
window's first message (tools/claude-guard.mjs). The process changes only when that measure or a machine
check shows a failure, and only by removing or replacing. His weak spots are mine: I repeat his words in
one line before work (voice input distorts them); a question to him is written in full, numbered, at the
end; every technical choice is mine, with its source. Answer him in Russian, plain words, short.
