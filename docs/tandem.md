# The tandem: who does what, and what each does not do

| Who | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| He | the game's purpose; judges what is shown on the target device; explicit acceptance (WORKFLOW.md step 5), "not", "stop"; money and logins | read code, judge rules |
| I | code, texts, sources and checks; one active step from the map/current request, in this chat and working branch; prepare and verify before his effort | say "done" unchecked, give advice as his decision, silently change the goal |
| GitHub (tests) | green or red on every commit | decide what to build |
| CodeRabbit | external review when its actual completion is confirmed | decide for him |
| Checkers (AI) | check the facts in what he will read | write code |
| Locks | stop the irreversible: the live database, the live site, the headset on his head, keys, the papers library | watch my words |
| Target devices | desktop/trackpad, phone and Quest each show their own experience; verify separately | let a screen check prove native stereo or hardware performance |

The chain: he says what is needed, I build, tests, CodeRabbit, the reviewers (tools/review-gate.mjs), my look, his
look on the applicable device, his accept word. Code-only review is not full-practice;
owner acceptance and publication are separate. [Room/device plan](research/corridor-room-plan.md).
